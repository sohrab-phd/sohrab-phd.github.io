About Me

I am an Artificial Intelligence researcher with interests in machine learning, deep neural networks, representation learning, and natural language processing. My research focuses on developing data-efficient and interpretable learning systems that can extract meaningful knowledge from complex textual and scientific data. My Master's research in Artificial Intelligence investigated transfer learning for relation extraction using transformer-based language models. This work explored how pre-trained representations can improve knowledge extraction under limited-data conditions and strengthened my interest in representation learning, transfer learning, and modern NLP. Alongside NLP research, I have applied machine learning and explainable AI techniques to interdisciplinary scientific problems, including spectroscopy-based classification and environmental modeling. These projects provided experience with high-dimensional data analysis, feature selection, model interpretability, and scientific machine learning.
